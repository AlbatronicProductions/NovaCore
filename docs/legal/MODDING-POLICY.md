3, 5, 7, 10 and 11. Root LICENSE controls a conflict unless it expressly delegates
the specific matter. Rights under 1.0 are not silently replaced by online edits.

Private personal core modifications are permitted. Original supported mods use
interfaces, formats and distribution means expressly designated by Licensor for
modding. No general SDK, current distribution service or ongoing compatibility is
promised. Calling a core/framework copy a mod does not authorize its distribution.

You may distribute original supported-mod files free through supported means.
Do not bundle NovaCore core source, binaries or extracted assets under this
permission. Independently licensed material requires its own permissions.
Your original mod contributions remain yours and may carry your own license;
that does not sublicense NovaCore. Recipients need their own lawful NovaCore rights.

Charging users for access, downloads, versions or updates requires separate
written authorization unless a later operative policy expressly permits it.
Voluntary donations unrelated to access are permitted. An access charge labelled
a donation remains paid access. Monetized creator content about mods follows the
[Creator Content Policy](CREATOR-CONTENT-POLICY.md).

Compensated development of a mod for a client/employer is permitted in itself,
including payment for development and delivery of original mod files to that
client/employer. It does not grant paid access to users, NovaCore redistribution
or commercial embedding rights. Those require their separate authorization.

Existing lawful, compliant, already-released free supported-mod versions may
remain hosted/downloadable and usable by compliant recipients after the author's
termination. The author may not issue new versions, updates or releases without
reinstatement or separate authorization. Never-compliant/infringing versions are
not protected. Later withdrawal of a supported designation does not alone revoke
existing compliant-version permissions; continuing technical support is not promised.
License section 10 controls survival and enforcement. Third-party and independently
applicable law/platform rights remain intact. Do not falsely imply endorsement.
