A zero-result query is only a search limit; it does not prove KSA has never had a limiter, device loss or a zoom crash. No historical entry examined ties excessive FPS to a whole-system GPU hang. Missing old causal details remain unknown rather than being reconstructed by analogy.

KSA history therefore strengthens the existing FIFO, per-image synchronization and bounded lifetime boundaries. It does not establish a NovaCore pacing deficiency, a thermal cause, or the safety of the M13.6 candidate after the reported timeout. Current installed IL must determine whether settings and helper code are actually reachable; a settings slider alone is not proof that a limiter executes.
