## What this does not prove

This is gross old-call work eligible for retirement/repositioning. Retained guards, new
readiness queries and numerical selection have unmeasured replacement cost. No claim of
net .080 savings, guaranteed percentiles or 0.05 median follows from this arithmetic.
K1 zero/denominator predicates still execute in admission; they are not measured free.
No equal split between K1 and K2 is invented. The one old 1.1168 ms tail remains unattributed.

Two independent source/cost reviews accepted this exact bounded closure. The conditional
steer permitted implementation; affected correctness and allocation passed. First fresh
Release control median **.0683 ms** failed **.05 ms**. That is the final performance result:
**REVISE; no correction #2, no later processes, no resumed broad qualification.**
