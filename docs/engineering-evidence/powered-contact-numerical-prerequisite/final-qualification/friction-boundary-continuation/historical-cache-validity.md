# Historical validity versus current initialization

The earlier basis-compatibility contract explicitly required strict positive normal support
and strict tangent/twist interior at both historical input and transformed output. It called
this a stricter prerequisite, not an upstream requirement. The predicate was subsequently
reused before D and after D by the consolidated operator.

| Operation | Does equality create a mathematical singularity? | Remaining responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Full-wrench basis transport | No friction slack enters its matrix | Same identities/features; valid geometry; rank, conditioning and impulse-error screens |
| Duration scaling | No; homogeneous positive scale preserves closed sets in real arithmetic | Exact producing/current duration; finite represented products; current recheck |
| Common-normal load correction | No slack division; denominator is mass/inertia/softness dependent | Correct old/new load; resulting normals/caps may shrink |
| D | No slack division; current q-transpose-A-q remains the denominator | Preserve current equations and arithmetic; final caps can change |
| Tangent/twist conversion | Included in the full-wrench map | Current geometry/radii may change feasibility |
| Cap computation | Defined at equality | Use current accumulated normals and correct four-row/radius weights |
| Old inactive-friction oracle/modal proof | General sliding boundary cannot be replaced by zero-slip equations | Independently qualified active reference and actual eight-sweep accuracy |

Strict interior was a deliberately narrower proof domain, not an algebraic necessity for
transport, duration, load or D. A zero-slip reference exactly at a cap may satisfy the map;
what is invalid is assuming all sliding saturated states satisfy the old interior equations.
Boundary admission alone does not extend an unclamped affine convergence proof.

The diagnostic therefore evaluates installed historical closed feasibility separately from
current initialization. Existing lineage, geometry, duration, body/load/support admission
and transport rank/conditioning screens remain. Basis/duration/load stage classifications
are recorded, not used as a premature final friction decision. Intermediate values must
remain arithmetically valid. Current closed feasibility and strict positive normals are
required after D and before any sweep. There is no clamp, cold fallback, or active-state
command. The solver remains free to transition active sets through its unchanged projection.

The historical inputs come from the prior independently checked baseline/powered tiny
installation receipts and their matching saved coast inputs. Each branch retains its own
kind/load/cache/geometry/duration. The diagnostic reconstructs the numerical owner from those
saved accepted tuples; it does not fabricate a new production capability, recreate a native
world, or claim a new live-world lifecycle qualification.

The generated diagnostic copies change admission sites and add observation/pause points only.
`generate.py` checks exact source occurrence counts and verbatim transport and solve/Bias
blocks. Prior candidate files remain untouched. This is not a permanent replacement of the
strict predicate. Final policy adoption depends on the bounded execution result.
