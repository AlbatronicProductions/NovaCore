## Map through the actual response contract

Current response law uses
k=1/m+(r cross n)^T I^-1(r cross n), J=-v_n/k,
DeltaV=J*n/m, DeltaL_body=J*(r_body cross n_body).

For spherical inertia and local r=x*t-h*n, incoming normal speed-u:
J=m*u*I/(I+m*x²),
omega=m*u*x/(I+m*x²),
Vplus_normal=-x*omega.
Tangential COM speed is unchanged by the normal impulse. Feature normal speed
is exactly zero in the ideal restitution-zero map; positive departure can arise
from curvature rather than artificial restitution.

M14.11 must fit actual impulse/velocity/angular-impulse interval requests. It does
not automatically refine a failing response enclosure. M14.12 tuple widths,
M14.13 represented-bit residual/energy/error admission, M14.14 pose widths and
M14.15 endpoint/quaternion remainder requirements are independent gates.
Mass and inertia cannot be treated as arbitrary desired post-impact states;
their effect must flow through these owners. Changing inertia/mass together can
preserve dimensionless response while changing absolute impulse widths, but no
such new source was proposed or executed in this assessment.

derivative, or inherited nonnegative initial derivative AND strictly positive
local second derivative. For a useful rotating-point case the latter must
survive actual Earth/represented-state/enclosure errors across a finite initial
interval. An infinitesimal represented residual is insufficient design margin.

After that initial node, clear-prefix boxes require positive gap or the actual
endpoint/derivative positivity predicates. Let E_g,E_v,E_a be PROVEN enclosure

Necessary screens include:
h*omega² > opposing curvature plus E_a,
x*peak(eta) > E_g,
x*omega*approach(eta) > E_v,
and signed beta endpoint gaps outside the relevant E_g.

error bound for a new source. Strong reserve over its OWN E_g must be established.

Beta-alpha, T-beta and the available initial-clear interval must comfortably
exceed the actual source-time and sign-resolution scales. A useful local
planning scale is E_g/v_min, where v_min is a certified approach lower bound;
actual endpoint signs, uniqueness and ordering are still required.
