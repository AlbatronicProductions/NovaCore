Canonical ownership, exact resources, commands, mass/COM/inertia, revision/history,
time/debt and atomic publication remain in NovaCore. BEPU owns subordinate retained
solver state only. The eight-iteration/one-substep ordinary contact policy is unchanged.
Source properties remain held through solving, followed by atomic canonical successor
and same-phase private acknowledgement. M15.2 free flight and M15.1 remain protected.

Precommit refusal does not invent rollback of an earlier accepted host input. Genuine
pending receipts support permitted publication retry without a second solve/debit.
Postcommit acknowledgement failure preserves actual canonical writes and invalidates
unsafe continuation. A consumer transfer never leaves two physical consumers eligible.

Stage 5 targets T(n)=T0+floor(n*1,000,000/60): 400 short and 800 long intervals.
StateRevision/history advance exactly 1,200; TimelineRevision does not. Equal total host
input at 30/60/150/240 Hz and the delayed schedule yields identical matching frontier
bits/history and exact debt conservation. Claims are same-build/machine only.

Historical source checkpoints and causal failures are linked from the retained
gauntlet directories. They are not additional current production configurations.
