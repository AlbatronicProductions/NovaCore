# Licensing strategy and policy authority

Recommended model: bespoke personal/modding/creator root terms, with explanatory
creator and modding companions and a separate commercial-information document.
The standard comparison is in [standard-license-evaluation.md](standard-license-evaluation.md).

Original NovaCore remains Albatronic-controlled. Intended grants cover personal
use and study, private personal core changes, supported mods, free distribution
of original mod files and monetized independent creator media. Core redistribution,
public forks, rebranding, paid mods and commercial framework embedding remain
separately authorized. Creator revenue does not become framework-sale permission.

Project Control explicitly answered both missing product-policy questions during
this correction ticket: permit private personal core changes; permit free original
supported-mod distribution; reserve public redistribution/commercial rights and
require authorization for paid mods. This resolves those product ambiguities.

Legal identity, patent treatment, warranty/liability, breach/cure, continuing media
rights, jurisdiction, contributions and hosting-platform interaction still require
review. These are exposed adoption questions, not assertions of approved legal
terms. The candidate is sufficient for review but not for operative publication.

All five `docs/legal/` files are **WITHHOLD PENDING PROJECT CONTROL / LEGAL REVIEW**.
NOTICE and LICENSING only state present ownership/status and can be proposed for
engineering banking. No root LICENSE exists or is proposed for automatic banking.
No public document needs to link to the withheld candidate.

Path A can bank engineering and status documents while withholding legal grants.
Path B can hold everything until exact legal terms receive approval. Technically,
Path A separates completed engineering from legal adoption; this is a recommendation
for consideration, not a decision. **Project Control must choose.**
