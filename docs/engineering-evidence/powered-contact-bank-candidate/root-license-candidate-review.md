# Root-license candidate review

Review target: `docs/legal/ROOT-LICENSE-CANDIDATE.md`. No current rights are granted.
The actual proposed clauses address scope/ownership, player/private source use,
supported mods, creator media/revenue, reserved framework rights, attribution,
third-party rights, warranty/liability, termination and companion/version precedence.

Legal-review ambiguities remain for platform-granted public-fork rights, mixed
commercial/personal use, mod donations linked to access, patent rights and mandatory
consumer laws. Do not treat the table as adjudicating legal exceptions. No proposal
narrows independent third-party or statutory rights. Clause 9's cure/continued-media
procedure is proposed and expressly unapproved, rather than hidden policy.

The bespoke candidate is not professionally validated. Recommendation: review the
actual text with counsel before adoption, including clause interaction and exact
companion versions. No root license or license grant enters the proposed bank.
