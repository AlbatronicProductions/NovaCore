## Expected effect on mass, COM and inertia

For store s with authored density measure rho_s(x,f), derive mass, centroid c_s and normalized central tensor J_s from its volume integrals. At zero remaining mass its contribution is zero, without dividing by zero. For transformed center r_s and orientation R_s, with P(r)=|r|^2*Identity-r*r^T:

`M = M_d + sum(m_s)`

`S = S_d + sum(m_s*r_s)`

`I_O = I_O,d + sum(m_s * (R_s*J_s*R_s^T + P(r_s)))`

`COM = S/M; I_COM = I_O - M*P(COM)`.
