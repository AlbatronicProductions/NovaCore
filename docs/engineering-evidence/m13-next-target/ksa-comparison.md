## Candidate A

`KSA.PlanetRenderer.cs:1770-1943` prepares the selected mesh's vertex data, procedural modifiers, final positions and normals, then barriers its persistent frame-indexed buffers for consumers. `KSA.Program.cs:4541` calls this responsibility during each rendered frame. There is no LOD/snap-only skip around this call or around its complete vertex dispatch. Persistent mesh topology, adjacency, buffer allocation and streamed texture views are reused; their contents are prepared for the current transforms and textures. `OnFrame:2143-2202` selects altitude LOD, ticks streaming, and builds camera/body matrices. `GetBillboardRotationTangentSnapped:2672-2715` retains a tangent reference and transports it across snapped movement, with a reset for large angular excursions. This is not a geographic-key physical-output cache.

The texture streamer's published views, frame indices, upload readiness and retirement queue govern texture/resource lifetime. That publication is not equivalent to NovaCore's complete authoritative residual footprint plus atomic CURRENT/incoming physical-generation publication. NovaCore must retain its stricter generation-4 authority and ready-before-draw boundary.

**ADAPT** the prepared-data/raster boundary and stable geographic sampling; both already exist. **INTENTIONALLY DIFFER** on update cadence: NovaCore stops preparation while stationary and copies same-direction vertices during a pupil refresh. Current KSA's whole-frame preparation is not evidence that NovaCore should regress this reuse or that a cross-index cache is already KSA-proven.

## Candidate B

`PlanetFrustumCulling.comp` performs conservative displaced-triangle sphere/frustum/planet/ocean tests, then writes a compact index list for indirect rendering. `TessellationFuncs.glsl:96-116` derives shared edge factors from projected edge size, perspective skew and distance; factors remain bounded to 1..64. `PlanetTessEvaluation.tese:77-89` returns prepared geometry outside its configured near range. Inside it, texture-driven displacement uses selected materials and biplanar sampling. Current helper comments explicitly leave backface/occlusion extensions as TODOs; they do not prove that KSA has eliminated tiny triangles or all invisible refinement. No matched KSA GPU timing was collected.

**ADOPT/ADAPT** conservative pre-refinement visibility, consistent shared edges and bounded near work, already represented in NCSM1. **INTENTIONALLY DIFFER** on physical data: NovaCore's analytic FP64 H and gradients cannot be replaced by KSA's texture displacement, material truncation or texture compression. No quality/range reduction is authorized. The current comparison does not establish a KSA production replacement that eliminates the measured NovaCore refinement delta unchanged.
