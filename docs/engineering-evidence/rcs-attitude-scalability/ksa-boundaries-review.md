## Important inference limits

No current KSA RCS-count cap or first-N gameplay policy appears in the inspected expansion/configuration/allocation/state/physical-output path. This establishes architecture scaling, not unlimited memory, measured KSA performance, or proof every unrelated KSA subsystem accepts every conceivable craft.

KSA Combustor.ConsumePropellant still discards the returned exact quantity/result of ResourceManager.MassChange. This review does not establish exact simultaneous shared-feed exhaustion. NovaCore must retain its stronger exact admission and physical-publication rules.

KSA's module order is collection/subtype-segment order and compaction can move slots. It is not a reference for NovaCore bitwise deterministic sorted identities. Stable IDs and deterministic reduction are positive NovaCore integration reasons for ADAPT.

## Smallest defensible NovaCore adaptation

Keep the authored graph -> immutable compiled all-actuator set -> deterministic demand/allocation -> service/resource admission -> realized force/torque chain. Replace fixed jet arrays/masks and gameplay refusal with count-sized owner-bound state/scratch created or grown at compile/runtime-admission boundaries. Use spans over the exact active count and explicit identity maps; do not encode jet identity in a 32-bit mask. Retain every authored actuator (including unavailable ones) in immutable identity; availability is a separate runtime result. Rebuild atomically replaces membership and workspaces, with no stale outputs. Preserve current physical support/load limits and independently measure 8/32/64/>64 counts.
