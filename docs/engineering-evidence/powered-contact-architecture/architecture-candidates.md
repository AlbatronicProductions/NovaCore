## Preferred responsibility chain

Canonical commands/source -> genuine engine/resource preparation -> one immutable authoritative physical interval -> one eligible private numerical consumer -> checked endpoint/mass/resource/actual successor -> one canonical transaction -> fixed private acknowledgement -> copied observation.

Remove duplicated expected-authority/actual/resource/host-publication ownership by factoring the minimum shared owner operations. Keep banked M14.17 clearance separate. Keep the M14.21 unpowered physical path and M15 qualified domain as regression references, not two production authorities.

No generalized vehicle regime framework, arbitrary callback dispatch, new public clock or second resource store is required.

## Why the production recommendation is currently REVISE

The exact source disproves a drop-in coupling:

1. Float contact time and reciprocal cannot execute the already-supported positive 2^-1075-second effect. Rejecting that domain silently or snapping to ticks is not convergence.
2. One installed mass per existing contact step does not prove continuous-mass constraint physics. Logarithmic mean matches a restricted free-force integral but fails as a universal support impulse model.
3. M15's physical admission excludes the asymmetric 1,000 kg/gravity article; unconstrained BEPU is not the M15 RK4 result. Consumer activation/recontact coverage also lacks a qualified path.

The **one bounded next proof** should settle an event-aware changing-mass constraint numerical operator, including its admitted model, exact-duration treatment, force/constraint sampling, finite-iteration impulse policy and zero-constraint reference comparison. No architecture wrapper can replace that proof.

Do not authorize the full production implementation from this report. Project Control may issue that bounded numerical qualification responsibility first; no prototype was created here.

## First production objective after that prerequisite

Choose **A — POWERED SUPPORTED CONTACT ONLY**, with departure explicitly deferred.

Proposed accomplishment wording: **Apply one exact finite-fuel powered interval to a retained supported spacecraft and jointly publish its successor.**

One authored fixed-COM dry body plus explicit central-point resource, one engine/full body wrench, one authored planar slab, fixed local frame/constant g, one retained world; original integer schedule and at most four canonical intervals per host call. Preserve the banked article definition rather than modifying its geometry/masses to fit M15. Any newly authored resource attachment and corresponding dry interpretation must be explicit and independently validated.

Allow canonical ignite/throttle/shutdown while supported. Select bounded centered/off-axis fixtures that remain supported during this first qualification. Do not clamp a genuinely lost support back into place. A departure/domain exhaustion must hold/report the last committed state and must not claim unqualified M15 continuation.

This is chosen because the source reveals a real free-consumer/domain/coverage transition, not because A is smaller by convention. Natural BEPU contact loss is easy; preserving accepted free-flight outcome and future recontact afterward is not yet proven.
