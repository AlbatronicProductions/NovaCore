## Convergence classifications

ADOPT means accept the equivalent responsibility. ADAPT means preserve that responsibility with explicit NovaCore ownership/types/applicability. INTENTIONALLY DIFFER refers to the specific mechanism named, not rejecting KSA as a whole. Source references resolve through [current-source-map](current-source-map.md); H numbers above are history, not current-code proof.

Positive NovaCore evidence for timing/authority differences: the [banked article formal result](../compound-contact-coverage/formal-qualification/README.md#host-schedules-and-physical-equivalence) reports equal bits at every one of 1,200 frontiers across 30/60/150/240 Hz and delayed service, exact ticks/debt/revisions/history, and retained private-only equivalence. `ContactServicingTests.cs:187–197` exercises external state/timeline/rate/pause mutation refusal; the retained [servicing report](../contact-episode-servicing/README.md) records those numerical gates, while its later status correction is separate. These are measured existing contracts to preserve, not proof that the proposed command system already passes.
