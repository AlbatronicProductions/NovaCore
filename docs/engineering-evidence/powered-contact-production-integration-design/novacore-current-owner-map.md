## Current failure and observation boundaries

Preparation can consume private engine edges/cursor without consuming canonical fuel. Resource proposal retirement can reprepare against a still-genuine engine parent; retiring the parent does not undo that cursor. Future contact retries must preserve this distinction.

Current free-flight publication consumes every lease on canonical success, including terminal acknowledgement failure. Current contact step invalidates before native mutation and restores validity only after complete successful export; ordinary canonical precommit refusal may retain a sound pending endpoint. These are existing reusable responsibilities, not yet a design for mass-update failure or powered contact publication.

M14.17 certified-clearance publication is a separate evidence/admission path. A retained BEPU receipt cannot supply its clearance evidence. No widening was proposed.

## Required delta visible from source

Current banked free-flight preparation directly invokes the free-flight evaluator; current contact service directly invokes fixed-source private stepping and paired publication. Neither path contains the accepted event mapper or a common mutually exclusive powered physical-consumer binding. Their canonical owner is already the same engine. A third spacecraft state owner is unnecessary. The common interval/consumer/acknowledgement design initially stopped at the mass gate; the subsequently authorized bounded investigation resolved that choice, and architecture design resumed.

Likely future integration surfaces, **not edits authorized now**: engine preparation/resource reads; physical consumer selection in engine servicing; retained world's numerical input/property admission; joint publication and expected-authority acknowledgement.
