## Source-proven command handoff gap

`CommitNextSpacecraftCommandCore` stores the latest engine edge/sequence at lines 158-160, shifts/removes its pending entry at 195-198, and returns the copied post-transition state at 199. `CloseSpacecraftCommandBoundary` verifies due commands are drained, then returns the final requested snapshot at 205-217. Therefore polling only the final snapshot cannot recover every same-boundary ignition/shutdown transition.

This is not a defect in M14.22's requested-state contract. A hardware consumer adds a new responsibility: bounded capture of committed transitions, before they cease to be observable. Capture must be prepared before command writes, under the same owner, with no callback. It needs backpressure: repeated commit/replenish can exceed seven transitions even though at most seven ordinary requests are pending at once. A final snapshot, numerical equality or CommandRevision alone cannot stand in for the ordered transition batch. See the proposed contract.
