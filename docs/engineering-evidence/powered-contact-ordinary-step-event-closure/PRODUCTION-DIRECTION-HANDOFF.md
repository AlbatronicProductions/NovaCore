## Why this direction won

The accepted bounded closure retains the exact event/resource ledger independently of BEPU's ordinary physical timestep, maps the admitted event into six consumed physical inputs, and leaves ordinary contact solving and cache ownership with retained BEPU. It avoids event-sized solver steps, event-duration cache rescaling, custom event-piece basis transport, common-load correction, D, 12-sweep event-piece policy and tiny-piece active-friction machinery **for this admitted closure**. This is not proof that every powered-contact regime is covered.

Literal current KSA shortage semantics were not adopted as NovaCore exact resource authority. Candidate C's chronological custom constraint operator was mathematically feasible but rejected for production advancement on payoff and retained-world integration burden. Candidate A remains a frozen fallback and historical reference, not universally obsolete. Its valid failures remain failures; see [Candidate A history](CANDIDATE-A-HISTORY.md).
