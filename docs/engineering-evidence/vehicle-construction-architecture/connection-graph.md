# Explicit topology

ConstructionDesignData reuses PartInstanceData, DefinitionReference and StructuralEdgeData. The optional connection extension has stable ID, detachable flag and explicit service mask. Legacy SRV rejects this extension and retains its schema/digest; both paths share rooted-parent validation and placement facts.

Structural ownership is a rooted connected acyclic tree, matching the inspected KSA PartTree. Extra service links are explicit independently owned edges; they may form cycles and carry directional propellant crossfeed. Electricity/data links are bidirectional. Endpoint capabilities intersect; structural attachment never silently grants a service. A used structural interface cannot acquire another structural mate, but explicitly authored service links may share an interface.

Definitions declare named rigid mating frames and compatibility families. Compile verifies the named pair and transforms within10 micrometres/1e-6 rotation-entry transport tolerance. Proximity never creates an edge. Snap solves parent pose × parent endpoint × opposing mate × inverse child endpoint. This bounded keyed connector contract does not invent a free-roll degree of freedom.

A detachable edge owns its child subtree. Partition() returns immutable membership only; it never creates bodies, changes stores, invokes decouplers or publishes successors. Resource/power proofs can omit a connection in separately compiled network facts in later stages.

DLV: nine parts/eight structural edges; two detachable interfaces pass Data only. Internal engine mounts explicitly carry Propellant and Data. Other generic fixtures opt into Electricity and Propellant. A service-cycle fixture proves the structural tree restriction does not forbid cyclic service connectivity.

Symmetry saves base/member IDs and each member's rigid transform relative to the base, with matching referenced definitions and exclusive group membership. Actions save ordered stage/module or detachable-edge references; they have no executor here.
