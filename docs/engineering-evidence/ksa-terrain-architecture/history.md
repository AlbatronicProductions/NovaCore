## Superseded-pattern verdict

1. **Confirmed architectural analogy, not the same defect:** resolving large
   terrain-domain precision at the consumer instead of carrying a precise local
   representation. KSA moved phase/anchor responsibility upstream. NovaCore uses
   correct FP64, not KSA's old inaccurate full FP32 direction, but pays for global
   reconstruction/domain reduction at final-sample frequency. A compatible cheaper
   domain evaluator is the best bounded lead; unchanged physical values and total
   performance remain unproved.
2. **Already converged:** base preparation before raster, reusable topology,
   indirect prepared-triangle culling, shared-edge perspective-aware tessellation,
   and the 50 m range. Do not sell these as new M13 gains.
3. **No confirmed match:** KSA retiring final hardware tessellation for cached
   refined physical meshes. Current source disproves that proposed history.
4. **No confirmed equivalent retired physical evaluator:** current KSA never
   demonstrates moving NovaCore's particular continuous near family/gradient out
   of TES while preserving those values. Its CPU/collision stack omits final
   material displacement. Similar appearance does not fill that proof gap.
5. **Separate responsibilities:** KSA's persistent rolling collision patches and
   impact prediction are genuine amortization, but not a rendered geometry cache.
   Its modifier shader refactor addressed compilation/specialization upstream,
   not millions of NovaCore final near-field samples.

These distinctions are why the chronology supports a precision-boundary proof,
but does not yet establish an implementation-ready large terrain migration.
