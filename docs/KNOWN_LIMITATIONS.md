# M16.0 — current limitations

**M16.0 is a banked internal engineering baseline. Player/public-release PASS is
UNASSIGNED; there is no packaged player release.** The
[current engineering state](NOVACORE_CURRENT_STATE.md) records the bank identity.
Earlier [campaign reports](engineering-evidence/performance-150fps/bank-candidate.md)
retain their historical pre-bank status and measurements. Banking preserves the
accepted engineering capabilities without closing the limits below.

- MinimumRecorder remains temporary developer/qualification infrastructure while
  historical blackout causality is **UNRESOLVED**. Its permanent public disposition
  remains **UNDECIDED — KEEP / DEV-ONLY / RETIRE**. The source bank retains necessary
  source and permanent tests for reproducibility; this is not acceptance of
  permanent public-player instrumentation. Runtime recorder data, raw journals,
  dumps/captures, bulk forensics and unselected generated evidence remain local-only
  and must never be committed or included in a player package.

- Latest [performance qualification](engineering-evidence/performance-150fps/README.md):
  Stage A CPU/contact closure is accepted, but stable 150 FPS at the saved 3440x1440 /
  actual 3440x1322 viewport remains OPEN. The final GPU payoff gate found no proven
  single sufficient safe correction; no new GPU implementation/exposure followed.
  Blackout cause remains unresolved; Player/public-release PASS remains unassigned.
- [Recorder storage](engineering-evidence/minimum-recorder-bounded-storage/README.md)
  now has a 512 MiB hard total cap, including metadata/capsules/indexes, with
  preventive maintenance at 400 MiB and full-session reservation before recording.
  Whole eligible sessions roll off automatically; older critical raw changes
  representation only through lossless validated preservation. Explicit
  RAW-PRESERVE, active, unknown or uncompressible unique evidence is kept.
  Routine fault-free ordinary summaries use a bounded rolling index; aged-out
  ordinary entries retain an aggregate receipt, not reconstructible history.
  If safe retirement cannot reserve recording, ordinary play may continue with
  an explicit unrecorded warning; mandatory-recorder qualification must refuse.

- Construction supports generic compatible depth within 1,024 parts and a
  4,000,000-byte document. Connector identity, occupancy and transforms belong to
  instances. The marker display holds 1,024 candidates with selected-host priority
  and an overflow message. Select a host when the view is crowded.
- Short/long articles and stock two-short/two-long-tank 64-jet articles are
  RCS lifecycle witnesses. There is no independent 32-jet ceiling. Launch still
  requires one main engine, realizable attitude authority, valid support,
  services, finite resources and other derived compiler/admission bounds.
  The current support revision adds a stock two-short-plus-long 96-jet flight
  witness. The earlier 96-jet RCS integration witness uses test-only half-capacity
  tanks; neither adds a seventh production part. See the current support evidence
  for final candidate qualification status.
  The 8-jet stock arrangement correctly refuses insufficient attitude authority.
  Larger CPU/scalability witnesses do not imply arbitrary flight qualification.
- Newly placed tanks and batteries are empty. **Fill consumables** fills tanks
  and charges batteries before Launch; Launch does not change the saved resource
  configuration. The launch-details dialog reports failed services or physical
  admission. A current starter RCS block contains four independently owned jets;
  additional valid rings expand the complete actuator collection. Physical
  support and authority refusals remain independent of actuator count.
- Physical flight runs at 1×. Celestial exploration warp is separate. Atmosphere,
  aerodynamic flight, staging, orbital insertion/return, reentry/heating,
  SAS/navball/autopilot and a complete orbit/atmospheric-return mission are not qualified.
- Generic launch support uses actual authored patches, native contact reactions
  and unchanged finite load ratings. There is no generic four-foot or tank-count
  envelope. Existing native preparation still bounds collision regions/vertices
  to 256 and admits upright coplanar support within the finite Florida slab and
  precision envelope; its 2,048-slice cold proof is not perpetual stability.
  Construction depth alone does not imply support, thrust or resource feasibility.
  Upper tanks do not automatically cross-feed the engine-connected tank. Heavy
  supported articles can require a long pad burn before T/W reaches one.
- The banked Surface Recontact implementation (see the historical [campaign](engineering-evidence/surface-recontact/README.md))
  supersedes the stabilization-era blanket terrain-impact refusal within its
  qualified domain: contact continuation, rocking/sliding/recontact, grounded
  commands and powered relaunch use the physical solver. Excessive/unqualified
  impacts still refuse continuation; structural damage/destruction is not implemented.
- RCS revision 2 separates solid hardware/mount clearance from the retired
  provisional expanding-plume editor veto. Revision-1 saves retain their original
  identities and are not silently upgraded. Use fresh craft for current tests.
- The normal product is a repository-layout development application. It requires
  resolved Git LFS assets, checked-in topology/elevation authority and two verified
  terrain-cache packages. It has no KSA or diagnostic-build dependency. Standalone
  installation and full diagnostic-web `dotnet publish` parity are not qualified.
- The completed blackout investigation retains its bounded native Debug evidence.
  This gauntlet separately qualifies current Release runtime/package behavior;
  it does not claim that historical Debug and current Release are byte-identical.
