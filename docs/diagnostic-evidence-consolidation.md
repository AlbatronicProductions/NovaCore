Unique bytes are not automatically permanent evidence. Unresolved responsibilities retain their output until resolution. Git/cache/compatibility debt remains separately owned.

The [per-investigation evidence index](engineering-evidence/earth-route-convergence/README.md) records resolution, causal conclusion, original report, representative visuals, reproduction source and raw/gross recovery sizes for all 19 completed investigation groups. The original failed/blocked reports are preserved as chronology; the accepted final visibility/material receiver correction and user manual PASS supersede their player-facing blockers. Unrelated broad-suite limits remain documented.

The package has a 160 MB budget. It retains reports and numerical summaries, input/runtime hashes and frame identities, diagnostic source/recipes, source recovery, selected contact sheets/key frames and seven automated representative videos. It also retains one exact 3440x1440, 120.512-second manual acceptance video confirmed by the user, plus its source hash and the PASS directive. The older cited failure video is absent from its original external path; its saved contact sheet, runtime log and original hash remain in the package. This limitation did not require retaining the resolved GPU-buffer corpus.

No raw depth/HDR/receiver/physical-vertex/TES/NPZ capture corpus remains. Old tooling may require a fresh bounded recapture and deliberate path/toolchain adaptation; absence of old buffers never constitutes a test pass. The archive does not promise bit-identical historical executable or frame regeneration.
